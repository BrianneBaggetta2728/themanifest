The Engine RoomBack of House

Ten engines, one Smartsheet workspace each. Engines never show their names to a Client or an employee; they publish contracts, and the Interfaces above render them. Each tile links to its Smartsheet workspace.

Why Smartsheet. Every engine's data lives in a Smartsheet workspace first, on purpose.Read more

Before anything appears in a prototype, its structure has to exist as a sheet: every entity, field, and picklist, filled with real rows. That is how we prove the model before anyone writes code, and it is why the Data Structure links below are the real thing, not a diagram. Smartsheet is the working model, never the production database. Dev rebuilds every one of these as a real schema, under one rule: every ratified row in the Shared Data Dictionary must map to a named element in the built system. If a row has no home in the code, the build is not done.
